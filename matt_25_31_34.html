Eschatological & Beatitude

Matt 25:31–34

The Eschatological Sense (Part 8)

English Scripture (Douay-Rheims Challoner)

And when the Son of man shall come in his majesty, and all the angels with him, then shall he sit upon the seat of his majesty (*super sedem maiestatis suae*)... Then shall the king say to them that shall be on his right hand: Come, ye blessed of my Father, possess you the kingdom prepared for you (*possidete paratum vobis regnum*) from the foundation of the world.

Biblia Sacra Vulgata

Cum autem venerit Filius hominis in maiestate sua, et omnes angeli cum eo, tunc sedebit super sedem maiestatis suae... Tunc dicet rex his qui ad dexteram eius erunt: Venite benedicti Patris mei, possidete paratum vobis regnum a constitutione mundi.

Thomistic Loci (*The Thomist* 2025 Standard): Super Mt., cap. 25, l. 3; ST III, q. 59, a. 5; In Orat., pet. 2.
St. Thomas Aquinas: Textus Latinus St. Thomas Aquinas: English Translation & Exegetical Commentary
1. Sensus Literalis (Literal Sense)
«Iudicium universale ubi Christus ut rex iudicat omnes gentes in carne sua gloriosa» (*Super Mt.*, cap. 25, l. 3). Authentic Latin Formula

The General Judgment: Christ the King publicly sits upon His glorious throne, judging all nations according to works of mercy.

Substantive Exegesis
2. Sensus Spiritualis (Spiritual / Allegorical / Tropological / Anagogical Sense)
«Possidete paratum vobis regnum: introductio electorum in perfectam fruitionem Dei in gloria Paradisi secundum opera misericordiae» (*In Orat.*, pet. 2). Authentic Latin Formula

The ultimate entrance of the elect into the everlasting Kingdom of Glory, eternally prepared for them by the predestination of the Father.

Substantive Exegesis
