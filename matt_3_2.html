Matt 3:2

The Evangelical Sense (Part 3)

English Scripture (Douay-Rheims Challoner)

Do penance: for the kingdom of heaven (*regnum caelorum*) is at hand.

Biblia Sacra Vulgata

Poenitentiam agite: appropinquavit enim regnum caelorum.

Thomistic Loci (*The Thomist* 2025 Standard): Super Mt., cap. 3, l. 1.
St. Thomas Aquinas: Textus Latinus St. Thomas Aquinas: English Translation & Exegetical Commentary
1. Sensus Literalis (Literal Sense)
«De adventu Christi in carnem, qui est initium regni Novi Testamenti» (*Super Mt.*, cap. 3, l. 1). Authentic Latin Formula

John the Baptist proclaims that the long-awaited Messianic era has arrived in the person of Christ.

Substantive Exegesis
2. Sensus Spiritualis (Spiritual / Allegorical / Tropological / Anagogical Sense)
«Regnum caelorum dicitur multipliciter: primo Christus ipse; secundo Sacra Scriptura; tertio Ecclesia militans; quarto iustitia fidei; quinto curia caelestis» (*Super Mt.*, cap. 3, l. 1). Authentic Latin Formula

Thomas outlines: (1) Christ Himself (*Autobasileia*), (2) Sacred Scripture / doctrine, (3) Church Militant, (4) Justice of faith in the soul (tropological), (5) Heavenly glory of the Church Triumphant (anagogical).
