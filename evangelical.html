Part 3

The Evangelical Sense

Lex Evangelii / Sacred Doctrine & Rule of Faith
Total Indexed Verses: 13 Loci
Scope: Canonical progression from Old Testament types to New Testament consummation

Thomistic Doctrinal Foundation

Secondly, St. Thomas teaches that the Kingdom of Heaven is the doctrine of Sacred Scripture and the Law of the Gospel (secundo dicitur Sacra Scriptura et doctrina evangelica, Super Mt., cap. 3, l. 1; ST I-II, q. 106, a. 1). The Kingdom of God comes through the proclamation of truth (Luke 4:43, Acts 28:31), which is the word of the kingdom sown in human hearts (Matt 13:19). Unlike the Old Law, which governed exterior acts through fear of temporal punishment, the Evangelical Law is an interior law written in the heart by the Holy Spirit (Lex Nova), ruling over the intellect and will through faith and charity.

Matt 4:17, 23
Evangelical & Christological

Literal: Christ opens His public ministry, authenticating the Gospel of the Kingdom by miracles of physical healing.

 For the kingdom of God is not in speech, but in power (*Non enim in sermone est regnum Dei, sed in virtute*). Super I Cor., cap. 4, l. 3.
