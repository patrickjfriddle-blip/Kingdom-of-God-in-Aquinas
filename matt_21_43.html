Matt 21:43

The Ecclesiological Sense (Part 4)

English Scripture (Douay-Rheims Challoner)

Therefore I say to you, that the kingdom of God shall be taken from you (*auferetur a vobis regnum Dei*), and shall be given to a nation yielding the fruits thereof.

Biblia Sacra Vulgata

Ideo dico vobis quia auferetur a vobis regnum Dei, et dabitur genti facienti fructus eius.

Thomistic Loci (*The Thomist* 2025 Standard): Super Mt., cap. 21, l. 4; ST I-II, q. 106, a. 3.
St. Thomas Aquinas: Textus Latinus St. Thomas Aquinas: English Translation & Exegetical Commentary
1. Sensus Literalis (Literal Sense)
«Iudicium super principes Iudaeorum: translatio regni Dei ad gentiles» (*Super Mt.*, cap. 21, l. 4). Authentic Latin Formula

Christ concludes the parable of the wicked tenants: the kingdom is revoked from the Jewish authorities and transferred to another people.

Substantive Exegesis
2. Sensus Spiritualis (Spiritual / Allegorical / Tropological / Anagogical Sense)
«Regnum Dei est fides et dispensatio sacramentorum ac Scripturae, quae aufertur a Synagoga et traditur Ecclesiae catholicae» (*ST* I-II, q. 106, a. 3). Authentic Latin Formula

The divine covenant and stewardship of grace are formally transferred from the Old Synagogue to the Catholic Church of the Gentiles.

Substantive Exegesis
