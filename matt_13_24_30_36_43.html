Ecclesiological & Eschatological

Matt 13:24–30, 36–43

The Ecclesiological Sense (Part 4)

English Scripture (Douay-Rheims Challoner)

The kingdom of heaven is likened (*Simile factum est regnum caelorum*) to a man that sowed good seed in his field... The Son of man shall send his angels, and they shall gather out of his kingdom (*de regno eius*) all scandals, and them that work iniquity... Then shall the just shine as the sun, in the kingdom of their Father (*in regno Patris eorum*).

Biblia Sacra Vulgata

Simile factum est regnum caelorum homini, qui seminavit bonum semen in agro suo... Mittet Filius hominis angelos suos, et colligent de regno eius omnia scandala, et eos qui faciunt iniquitatem... Tunc iusti fulgebunt sicut sol in regno Patris eorum.

Thomistic Loci (*The Thomist* 2025 Standard): Super Mt., cap. 13, ll. 2, 4; ST II-II, q. 10, a. 8; ST III, q. 8, a. 3.
St. Thomas Aquinas: Textus Latinus St. Thomas Aquinas: English Translation & Exegetical Commentary
1. Sensus Literalis (Literal Sense)
«Parabola zizaniorum: mixtio bonorum et malorum in agro mundi usque ad messem finis saeculi» (*Super Mt.*, cap. 13, l. 2). Authentic Latin Formula

Parable of the Weeds: good seed and weeds co-exist in the field until the harvest at the close of the age.

Substantive Exegesis
2. Sensus Spiritualis (Spiritual / Allegorical / Tropological / Anagogical Sense)
«Regnum Christi in praesenti est Ecclesia militans continens malos et bonos; in futuro purgabitur ab omni scandalo et transferetur in regnum Patris» (*Super Mt.*, cap. 13, l. 4). Authentic Latin Formula

Refutes Donatism: 'His kingdom' in this age is the Church Militant as a mixed body (*corpus permixtum*); at the Last Judgment, sinners are separated, and the just enter the Kingdom of Glory.
