Project Overview & Hermeneutical Architecture

This interactive scholarly database constitutes the biblical and speculative foundation for the thesis The Kingdom of God and the Kingdom of Man in St. Thomas Aquinas. Addressing the historical and theological debates on the kingship of Christ and the dual jurisdictions of Church and State, this research unifies Aquinas’s scriptural commentaries (Super Evangelium S. Matthaei, Super Ioannem, Super Epistolas S. Pauli, Super Psalmos) with his speculative masterpieces (Summa Theologiae, Scriptum super Sententiis, and De regno ad regem Cypri).

Gen 49:10
Christological Type
 The sceptre (*sceptrum*) shall not be taken away from Juda, nor a ruler (*dux*) from his thigh, till he come that is to be sent, and he shall be the expectation of nations. ST III, q. 31, a. 2; Super Is., cap. 11; Super Io., cap. 1, l. 14.
Literal: Jacob prophesies the continuity of the civil and judicial sceptre of Judah until the historical advent of the Messiah.
📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔
Spiritual: The cessation of the earthly sceptre signifies the transition from the temporal Old Law to the universal spiritual kingdom of Christ among all Gentile nations.

Exod 15:18
Cosmic & Eschatological
 The Lord shall reign for ever and ever (*Dominus regnabit in aeternum et ultra*). Super Ps. 9, n. 4; Super Ps. 28, n. 4; ST I, q. 103, a. 1.
Literal: Moses celebrates YHWH's sovereign victory over Pharaoh's hosts at the Red Sea as proof of His perpetual kingship.
📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔
Spiritual: The Red Sea prefigures Baptism, following which God establishes His everlasting kingdom over the elect in eternal beatitude.

Ecclesiological Type
 And you shall be to me a priestly kingdom (*regnum sacerdotale*), and a holy nation (*gens sancta*). ST I-II, q. 104, a. 1, ad 1; ST III, q. 22, a. 1, ad 3.
Literal: God constitutes the historical commonwealth of Israel as a dedicated theocracy under the Sinai covenant.
📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔
Spiritual: Prefigures the Church Militant of the New Law, where all the baptized participate sacramentally in Christ's eternal royal priesthood (*regnum sacerdotale*).

Num 23:21
Mystical Type
 The Lord his God is with him, and the sound of the victory of the king (*clangor victoriae regis*) in him. ST I-II, q. 102, a. 4; Super Ps. 46, n. 2.
Literal: Balaam confesses that YHWH is present as the supreme royal Leader and Victor among the camps of Israel.
📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔
Spiritual: Signifies the indwelling of God in the soul of the just, whose grace grants continuous triumph over the assaults of demonic powers.

Deut 33:5
Cosmic / Political Type
 He shall be king (*rex*) with the most right, the princes of the people being assembled with the tribes of Israel. ST I-II, q. 105, a. 1.
Literal: God is proclaimed the supreme legislative King of Jeshurun, establishing an optimal mixed regime under divine law.
📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔
Spiritual: Typifies Christ the supreme Lawgiver assembling the princes of the Church and all peoples into the unity of faith.

Judg 8:23
Cosmic / Theocratic Type
 Gedeon said to them: I will not rule over you, neither shall my son rule over you, but the Lord shall rule over you (*sed dominabitur vobis Dominus*). ST I-II, q. 105, a. 1, ad 2; De reg. I, cap. 2.
Literal: Gideon rejects hereditary human monarchy, maintaining the unmediated theocratic sovereignty of YHWH over Israel.
📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔
Spiritual: The human will must remain subject immediately to divine rule, refusing to allow created appetites or vices to exercise dominion over the soul.
📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔
1 Sam 8:7
Cosmic / Theocratic Type
 And the Lord said to Samuel: Hearken to the voice of the people in all that they say to thee. For they have not rejected thee, but me, that I should not reign over them (*ne regnem super eos*). ST I-II, q. 105, a. 1, ad 2; De reg. I, cap. 1.
Literal: Israel's sin was desiring an earthly king after the manner of the Gentiles, thereby discounting God's immediate royal protection.
📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔
Spiritual: Prefigures the human soul defecting from the gentle internal governance of grace to seek external, perishable consolations.

1 Sam 12:12
Cosmic Type

Literal: Samuel rebukes Israel's lack of faith during Nahash's invasion, recalling that God Himself was their true King.
📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔

2 Sam 7:12–16
Christological Type
 I will establish the throne of his kingdom for ever (*firmabo thronum regni eius usque in sempiternum*)... And thy house shall be firm, and thy kingdom for ever (*regnum tuum usque in sempiternum*) before thy face, and thy throne shall be firm for ever. ST III, q. 31, a. 2; ST III, q. 59, a. 2; Super Heb., cap. 1, l. 3; Super Ps. 88, n. 4.
Literal: The Nathan oracle promises an enduring dynasty; because Solomon's earthly kingdom fell, Thomas states the literal sense intended by the Holy Spirit terminates strictly in Christ (*sensus litteralis propheticus*).
📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔
Spiritual: The throne of Christ established permanently in the faithful soul by charity, perfected in the eternal kingdom of glory.
