Christological

Isa 6:1–5

The Christological Sense (Part 2)

English Scripture (Douay-Rheims Challoner)

I saw the Lord sitting upon a throne high and elevated (*sedentem super solium excelsum et elevatum*)... and my eyes have seen the King the Lord of hosts (*regem Dominum exercituum*).

Biblia Sacra Vulgata

Vidi Dominum sedentem super solium excelsum et elevatum... et regem Dominum exercituum vidi oculis meis.

Thomistic Loci (*The Thomist* 2025 Standard): Super Is., cap. 6; Super Io., cap. 12, l. 7; ST I, q. 12, a. 11.
St. Thomas Aquinas: Textus Latinus St. Thomas Aquinas: English Translation & Exegetical Commentary
1. Sensus Literalis (Literal Sense)
«Isaias vidit Deum sub specie regis sedentis in maiestate sua in templo» (*Super Is.*, cap. 6). Authentic Latin Formula

Isaiah beholds a majestic theophany of YHWH the King enthroned in the celestial sanctuary.

Substantive Exegesis
2. Sensus Spiritualis (Spiritual / Allegorical / Tropological / Anagogical Sense)
«Haec dixit Isaias quando vidit gloriam eius, scilicet Christi, et locutus est de eo» (*Super Io.*, cap. 12, l. 7). Authentic Latin Formula

John 12:41 identifies this vision as the pre-incarnate glory of Christ, revealing the eternal kingship of the Son.
