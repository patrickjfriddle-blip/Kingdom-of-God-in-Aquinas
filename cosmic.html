Part 6

The Cosmic & Theocratic Sense

Dominium naturale / Universal Providence & Political Sovereignty
Total Indexed Verses: 18 Loci
Scope: Canonical progression from Old Testament types to New Testament consummation

Thomistic Doctrinal Foundation

Fifthly, the Kingdom of God encompasses His universal, providential dominion over all creation and human commonwealths (Dominium naturale super omnia, ST I, q. 103, aa. 1–3). Grounded in the Old Testament theocracy (Exod 15:18, 1 Sam 8:7) and the Enthronement Psalms (Ps 92, 98, 102, 144), God is the supreme King who directs all nature and secular rulers (Prov 8:15–16, De regno I, cap. 1). All legitimate temporal power participates in and is derived from this cosmic divine sovereignty, possessing its own authentic natural end while remaining subordinate to divine eternal law.

Exod 15:18
Cosmic & Eschatological
 The Lord shall reign for ever and ever (*Dominus regnabit in aeternum et ultra*). Super Ps. 9, n. 4; Super Ps. 28, n. 4; ST I, q. 103, a. 1.
Literal: Moses celebrates YHWH's sovereign victory over Pharaoh's hosts at the Red Sea as proof of His perpetual kingship.

Spiritual: The Red Sea prefigures Baptism, following which God establishes His everlasting kingdom over the elect in eternal beatitude.

Deut 33:5
Cosmic / Political Type
 He shall be king (*rex*) with the most right, the princes of the people being assembled with the tribes of Israel. ST I-II, q. 105, a. 1.
Literal: God is proclaimed the supreme legislative King of Jeshurun, establishing an optimal mixed regime under divine law.
📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔
Spiritual: Typifies Christ the supreme Lawgiver assembling the princes of the Church and all peoples into the unity of faith.

Judg 8:23
Cosmic / Theocratic Type
 Gedeon said to them: I will not rule over you, neither shall my son rule over you, but the Lord shall rule over you (*sed dominabitur vobis Dominus*). ST I-II, q. 105, a. 1, ad 2; De reg. I, cap. 2.
Literal: Gideon rejects hereditary human monarchy, maintaining the unmediated theocratic sovereignty of YHWH over Israel.
📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔
Spiritual: The human will must remain subject immediately to divine rule, refusing to allow created appetites or vices to exercise dominion over the soul.

1 Sam 8:7
Cosmic / Theocratic Type
 And the Lord said to Samuel: Hearken to the voice of the people in all that they say to thee. For they have not rejected thee, but me, that I should not reign over them (*ne regnem super eos*). ST I-II, q. 105, a. 1, ad 2; De reg. I, cap. 1.
Literal: Israel's sin was desiring an earthly king after the manner of the Gentiles, thereby discounting God's immediate royal protection.
📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔
Spiritual: Prefigures the human soul defecting from the gentle internal governance of grace to seek external, perishable consolations.

1 Sam 12:12
Cosmic Type
 You said to me: Nay, but a king shall reign over us (*rex imperabit nobis*): whereas the Lord your God was your king (*cum Dominus Deus vester regnaret in vobis*). ST I-II, q. 105, a. 1, ad 2; Super I Cor., cap. 15, l. 3.
Literal: Samuel rebukes Israel's lack of faith during Nahash's invasion, recalling that God Himself was their true King.
📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔
Spiritual: Rebukes the believer who relies upon secular power or carnal strength in spiritual warfare instead of the Lord.

📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔

Cosmic & Christological
 The Lord hath reigned (*Dominus regnavit*), he is clothed with beauty: the Lord is clothed with strength, and hath girded himself... Thy throne is prepared from of old (*Parata sedes tua ex tunc*): thou art from everlasting. ST I, q. 103, a. 1; In Orat., pet. 2. (Super Ps. 92 deest).
Literal: Enthronement psalm declaring the primordial majesty, beauty, and unshakeable antiquity of God's universal reign.

Prov 8:15–16
Cosmic / Political
 By me kings reign (*Per me reges regnant*), and lawgivers decree just things, by me princes rule, and the mighty decree justice. ST I-II, q. 93, a. 3; De reg. I, cap. 1; Sent. II, d. 44, q. 1, a. 2.
Literal: Divine Wisdom is the foundation of all civil authority, so that temporal monarchs rule justly only by participating in Eternal Law.
📖 Aquinas Latin & English Text ➔
Spiritual: The infused gift of Wisdom enables the believer to govern his own soul, exercising true moral kingship over his internal appetites.
