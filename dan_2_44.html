Christological & Eschatological

Dan 2:44

The Christological Sense (Part 2)

English Scripture (Douay-Rheims Challoner)

But in the days of those kingdoms the God of heaven will set up a kingdom that shall never be destroyed (*suscitabit Deus caeli regnum, quod in aeternum non dissipabitur*), and his kingdom shall not be delivered up to another people (*regnum eius alteri populo non tradetur*), and it shall break in pieces, and shall consume all these kingdoms, and itself shall stand for ever.

Biblia Sacra Vulgata

In diebus autem regnorum illorum suscitabit Deus caeli regnum, quod in aeternum non dissipabitur, et regnum eius alteri populo non tradetur: comminuet autem, et consumet universa regna haec, et ipsum stabit in aeternum.

Thomistic Loci (*The Thomist* 2025 Standard): Super Mt., cap. 21, l. 4; ST III, q. 59, a. 2; De reg. I, cap. 14.
St. Thomas Aquinas: Textus Latinus St. Thomas Aquinas: English Translation & Exegetical Commentary
1. Sensus Literalis (Literal Sense)
«Lapis abscissus sine manibus est Christus natus de Virgine, destruens regna terrena et erigens regnum sempiternum» (*Super Mt.*, cap. 21, l. 4). Authentic Latin Formula

Daniel interprets the stone cut without hands shattering the metallic statue as an unshakeable kingdom established by God during the fourth empire.

Substantive Exegesis
2. Sensus Spiritualis (Spiritual / Allegorical / Tropological / Anagogical Sense)
«Regnum Christi alteri populo non tradetur, quia Ecclesia manet usque ad finem saeculi et transfertur in gloriam» (*De reg.* I, cap. 14). Authentic Latin Formula

The Stone is Christ born of a Virgin, who during the Roman Empire founded a spiritual kingdom that subdues earthly empires and stands for all eternity.
