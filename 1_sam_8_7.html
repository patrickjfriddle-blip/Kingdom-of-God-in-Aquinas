1 Sam 8:7

The Cosmic & Theocratic Sense (Part 6)

English Scripture (Douay-Rheims Challoner)

And the Lord said to Samuel: Hearken to the voice of the people in all that they say to thee. For they have not rejected thee, but me, that I should not reign over them (*ne regnem super eos*).

Biblia Sacra Vulgata

Dixit autem Dominus ad Samuelem: Audi vocem populi in omnibus quae loquuntur tibi: non enim te abiecerunt, sed me, ne regnem super eos.

Thomistic Loci (*The Thomist* 2025 Standard): ST I-II, q. 105, a. 1, ad 2; De reg. I, cap. 1.
St. Thomas Aquinas: Textus Latinus St. Thomas Aquinas: English Translation & Exegetical Commentary
1. Sensus Literalis (Literal Sense)
«Peccaverunt petendo regem ad modum gentium, quasi diffidentes de divina providentia et regimine» (*ST* I-II, q. 105, a. 1, ad 2). Authentic Latin Formula

Israel's sin was desiring an earthly king after the manner of the Gentiles, thereby discounting God's immediate royal protection.

Substantive Exegesis
2. Sensus Spiritualis (Spiritual / Allegorical / Tropological / Anagogical Sense)
«Apostasia voluntatis a regimine divino ad dominatum carnalem et saecularem» (*De reg.* I, cap. 1). Authentic Latin Formula

Prefigures the human soul defecting from the gentle internal governance of grace to seek external, perishable consolations.
